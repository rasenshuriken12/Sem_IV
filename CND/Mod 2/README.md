# Physical Layer & Data Link Layer

### Transmission Media

- Guided Media
  - Twisted Pair
  - Co-axial Cable
  - Optical Fiber
- Unguided Media

### Twisted Pair Cable 

- A twisted pair cable contains:
  - Two copper conductors
  - Each wire is covered with insulation
  - The wires are twisted around each other

- Characteristics
  - Twisting helps reduce electromagnetic interference (EMI) and crosstalk.
  - Usually used for short-distance communication.
  - Has lower bandwidth than fiber optic cables.

- Applications
  - Telephone communication systems
  - Local Area Networks (LAN)
  - Security and surveillance systems

| Pros | Cons |
|--|--|
| Low cost compared to other transmission media. | Limited bandwidth compared to fiber optic cables.
| Easy to install and maintain. | Shorter transmission distance (about 100 meters without repeaters).

- Near-End Crosstalk (NEXT) occurs when a signal transmitted in one wire pair interferes with another pair in the same cable, and the interference is detected at the transmitting end.

| No. | UTP (Unshielded Twisted Pair) | STP (Shielded Twisted Pair) |
|----|--------------------------------|-----------------------------|
| 1 | No additional shielding around the twisted wires | Has a metal shield (foil or braided) around the wires |
| 2 | Less protection against electromagnetic interference (EMI) | Better protection against electromagnetic interference |
| 3 | Cheaper and widely used | More expensive |
| 4 | Easier to install and maintain | Harder to install due to shielding |
| 5 | Lighter and more flexible | Heavier and less flexible |

### Co-axial Cable
- has a central copper conductor surrounded by insulation, metallic shielding, and an outer protective jacket. The shielding protects the signal from electromagnetic interference (EMI).

- Characteristics
  - Provides better protection from electromagnetic interference than twisted pair.
  - Can transmit signals over longer distances.
  - Supports higher bandwidth than twisted pair cables.

- Applications of Coaxial Cable
  - Cable television networks
  - Broadband internet connections

| Pros | Cons |
|--|--|
| Higher bandwidth than twisted pair cables. | More expensive than twisted pair cables
| Longer transmission distance. | Harder to install and maintain
